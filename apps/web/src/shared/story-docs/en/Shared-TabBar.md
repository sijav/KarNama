The phone's navigation, across the foot of the screen: the same three places as
the sidebar, the board, the stats and the network, in equal thirds, the board
first.

The current place is blue. Nothing else joins the bar: on a phone adding a job
opportunity is the board's header button, and the language and signing out are
inside the settings the header's gear opens.

## Props

### current

The place the reader is on: `jobs`, `stats` or `network`. With `add`, which
the bar does not draw, none is marked.

### onNavigate

Called with the place pressed.

## Stories

### Default

The bar as the design draws it, measured, a place pressed.

### InEnglish

The bar in English, the board on the left.

### StatsCurrent

The bar on the Stats page.

### NetworkCurrent

The bar on the network page.

### Dark

The bar in the dark scheme, in the theme's dark colours.
