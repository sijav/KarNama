The month calendar's turn to the month before or after.

A small chevron in grey that points the way the days run: the next month's
points to the end of the line and the previous month's to its start, so in
Persian the next month's points left. Under the pointer its square turns light
grey.

## Props

### direction

Which month it turns to, `next` or `previous`.

### onClick

Called when the arrow is pressed, by pointer or by keyboard.
