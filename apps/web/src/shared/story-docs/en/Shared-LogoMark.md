The product's mark, a K made of a capsule and two leaves, as the design draws it
at 32 by 32: the capsule and the upper leaf in the brand's blue, the lower leaf
in a light grey. It sits beside the product's name in the sidebar and on the
sign-in and sign-up screens, and follows the colour scheme through the theme.

It is decoration by default and hidden from screen readers, because the name
beside it already says what it is. Given a name, it is announced as an image.

## Props

### aria-label

The mark's name for screen readers, for a place where it stands without the
product's name beside it. Leave it out when the name is next to it.

## Stories

### Default

The mark in Persian, measured against the design: the 32 square, the three
shapes of the drawing, and their two colours.

### InEnglish

The same mark in English. A mark has no direction, so it is not mirrored: the
capsule stays on the left in either language.

### Dark

The mark in the dark scheme, in the theme's dark colours.

### Named

The mark with the product's name, announced as an image.
