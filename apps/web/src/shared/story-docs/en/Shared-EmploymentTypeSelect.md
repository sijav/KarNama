The job record's employment type: a select of the eight types, more than one at
a time, since a job can be full-time and remote at once.

The types are the owner's list of 2026-09-10: the six the design draws, in its
order, then freelance and temporary.

## Props

### value

The types chosen, as a list.

### disabled

When set, the field cannot be opened.

### onChange

Called with the new list of types after each choice.

## Stories

### Default

The field with full-time chosen and its eight types in Persian.

### InEnglish

The same in English.
