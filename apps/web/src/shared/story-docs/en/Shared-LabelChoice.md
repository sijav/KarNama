One relationship label in the person modal's label picker.

A pill with its label in grey; under the pointer it turns light grey. A chosen
label is light blue with a blue edge, its name in blue after a tick. The Add
choice, «New label», has a dashed edge and a plus, in blue, and starts a new
label.

## Props

### label

The label's name, in the reader's language for a default and as the reader
wrote it for their own.

### selected

Draws the chosen label.

### add

Draws the dashed «New label» choice, which is a button rather than a label.

### onClick

Called when the choice is pressed, by pointer or by keyboard.
