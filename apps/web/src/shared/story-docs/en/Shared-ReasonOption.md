One reason a job opportunity is being deleted, as a row in the delete window's
group of reasons: a radio at the start of the row and the reason after it. The
whole row can be pressed. At rest it is white with a light grey edge; under the
pointer the edge darkens; chosen, the row turns a light blue inside a blue edge,
and its radio fills with blue around a white centre.

## Props

### value

The reason's id, which the group reports when it is chosen.

### label

The reason, as the reader reads it.

## Stories

### Default

A reason not chosen, measured against the design.

### Hover

Under a real pointer, the darker edge. In Storybook itself, hover it yourself.

### Selected

The chosen reason, light blue inside its blue edge.

### SelectedHover

The chosen reason under a real pointer, its edge darker.

### Choosing

A press anywhere on the row chooses it.

### KeyboardFocus

Reached by Tab, the row draws the focus ring round itself.

### InEnglish

The chosen reason in English, its radio at the left.

### Dark

The chosen reason in the dark scheme, in the theme's dark colours.
