One month to pick a day on.

A Persian reader's month is the Solar Hijri one and an English reader's the
Gregorian, as every other date in the product is written. The week starts on
Saturday in both, and Friday is red. The header has the previous month's arrow
at the start, the month's name and year in the middle, and the next month's
arrow at the end. Under it are the weekdays' letters and then the month's weeks,
five or six, which fade in when the month turns.

Today has a blue ring, the chosen day is blue, and the days the field does not
allow are pale and take no press. On the keyboard one day is in the Tab order;
the arrows move a day, or a week up and down, Home and End go to the week's
first and last days, Page Up and Page Down go a month back and forward, and a
move past the month turns it.

## Props

### today

Today's date, which is ringed.

### selected

The chosen date, drawn blue. The month it is in shows first, and the month
turns to it when it moves outside, as a shortcut moves it.

### min

The first day the field allows; the days before it are off.

### max

The last day the field allows; the days after it are off.

### initialMonth

A day whose month shows first, before the chosen day's or today's.

### onSelect

Called with the date of the day pressed, unless it is off.
