One number on the first row of the Stats page, in a light grey card: a small
dot in the colour that status has on the board, the label after it in dark
grey, and under them the number, large and black, in the reader's own digits.
The five count the applied, the interviews, the offers, the rejected and the
job opportunities that never had an answer, whose dot is grey. The card fills
the width its row gives it.

The dot is decoration; the label says what is counted.

## Props

### status

Which number it is, and so the dot's colour: `applied`, `interview`, `offer`,
`rejected` or `no-response`.

### label

What it counts, written beside the dot.

### value

The count.

## Stories

### Default

The applied count measured against the design.

### EveryStatus

All five, each with its own dot.

### ALargeNumber

A count in the thousands, grouped as the reader groups it.

### InEnglish

The same card in English, with Latin digits at the left.

### Dark

The card in the dark scheme, in the theme's dark colours.
