The dashed «+ افزودن نفر» row that ends a job opportunity's related people, which
adds a person to it. As wide as what holds it, with a plus and the label in the
brand's blue centred inside a dashed grey edge; on hover it fills with a light
grey.

The design draws it no focus of its own, so keyboard focus draws the three pixel
blue ring inside the edge that the icon buttons draw.

## Props

### onClick

Called when the row is pressed, by pointer or by keyboard, to add a person.

## Stories

### Default

The row in Persian at the related people tab's width, measured against the
design, and pressed.

### InEnglish

The same row in English.

### Dark

The row in the dark scheme, in the theme's dark colours.

### Hover

The pointer over the row: the light grey fill.

### KeyboardFocus

The row reached with Tab, wearing the focus ring.
