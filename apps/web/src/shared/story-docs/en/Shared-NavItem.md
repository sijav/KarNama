One row of the sidebar's navigation: an icon and the name of a place.

The row at rest is quiet; the pointer puts a pale ground behind it, and the
place the reader is on is blue on a pale blue ground and is announced as the
current page. The keyboard's focus draws a ring inside the row.

## Props

### icon

The icon at the row's start.

### label

The name of the place.

### active

Whether this is the page the reader is on.

### onClick

Called when the row is pressed.

## Stories

### Default

The row at rest, measured against the design.

### Active

The row of the current page.

### Hover

The row under the pointer.

### Focus

The row focused by the keyboard, its ring inside.

### InEnglish

The row in English, the icon on the left.
