A small answer the reader gives with one press, as the interview question's
«دور بعدی», «منتظر نتیجه» and «نتیجه گرفتم». The label sits in the brand's
blue at 12 inside a thin grey edge with slightly rounded corners; on hover the
fill turns a light grey and the edge darkens.

The design draws it no focus of its own, so keyboard focus draws the same
three pixel blue ring inside the edge that the icon buttons draw.

## Props

### label

The answer the chip gives, in the reader's language.

### onClick

Called when the chip is pressed, by pointer or by keyboard.

## Stories

### Default

The chip in Persian at rest, measured against the design, and pressed.

### InEnglish

The same chip in English.

### Dark

The chip in the dark scheme, in the theme's dark colours.

### Hover

The pointer over the chip: the light grey fill and the darker edge.

### KeyboardFocus

The chip reached with Tab, wearing the focus ring.
