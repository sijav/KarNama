The two-way switch on the Stats page's applications card, daily or weekly: the
chosen period's word on white in the main text colour, the other in grey, both
on a light grey track. There is no monthly. Which comes first is the page's to
say, from the reader's first application; the switch shows what it is told and
asks for the other when it is chosen.

It is one group of two radios: Tab reaches the chosen one, the arrow keys move
to the other and choose it, the left and right arrows going the way they point
in either language, and keyboard focus draws a blue ring inside the pill.

## Props

### value

The period shown as chosen, `daily` or `weekly`.

### onChange

Called with the other period when the reader chooses it, by pointer or by the
arrow keys.

## Stories

### Daily

Daily chosen, in Persian, measured against the design.

### Weekly

Weekly chosen.

### InEnglish

The switch in English, daily on the left.

### Dark

The switch in the dark scheme, in the theme's dark colours.

### ChoosingByPress

A press on the chosen period asks for nothing; a press on the other asks for it.

### ChoosingByArrows

Tab reaches the chosen period, with the focus ring, and the left arrow chooses
the period to its left.
