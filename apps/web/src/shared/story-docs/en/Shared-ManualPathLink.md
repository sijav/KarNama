The line under the add form's paste field for a reader with nothing to paste:
«لینک یا متن آگهی ندارید؟», No link or posting text?, in grey, and after it
«ورود دستی», Manual entry, in blue, which opens the empty form to fill in by
hand. The link is underlined under the pointer, and keyboard focus draws a
blue outline round it.

## Props

### onClick

Called when the link is pressed, by pointer or by keyboard.

## Stories

### Default

The line measured against the design, and the link pressed.

### Hover

The pointer over the link: it is underlined.

### KeyboardFocus

Reached by Tab, with the blue outline.

### InEnglish

The same line in English, the question at the left.

### Dark

The line in the dark scheme, in the theme's dark colours.
