A day the date dropdown offers at one press, «Today», «Yesterday» or «Two days
ago».

A small grey pill with its label in dark grey; under the pointer the pill turns
a little darker. The Persian three are as wide as each other, and an English
label grows.

## Props

### label

The day it offers, in the reader's language.

### onClick

Called when the shortcut is pressed, by pointer or by keyboard.

## Stories

### Default

The shortcut in Persian, measured against the design.

### Hover

The shortcut under the pointer.

### Pressed

The shortcut pressed.

### InEnglish

The shortcut in English.

### Dark

The shortcut in the dark scheme, in the theme's dark colours.
