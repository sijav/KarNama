An empty column with a dashed grey edge that makes a new status, «+ افزودن
وضعیت», Add status. It is as wide as a status column, 300, and always stands
beside the open stages; the plus and the words are centred in it in the
secondary text colour, and a light grey ground shows under the pointer.

Keyboard focus draws a blue ring inside its edge.

## Props

### onAdd

Called when it is pressed, by pointer or by keyboard.

## Stories

### Default

The column measured against the design, and pressed.

### Hover

The pointer over it: the light grey ground inside the same dashed edge.

### KeyboardFocus

Reached by Tab, with the blue ring.

### InEnglish

The same column in English, the plus on the left.

### Dark

The column in the dark scheme, in the theme's dark colours.
