The dashed «+ افزودن وضعیت» at the end of the status picker's open stages, which
makes a new stage. A plus and the label in the brand's blue inside a pill with a
dashed blue edge; on hover the pill fills with a light grey.

The design draws it no focus of its own, so keyboard focus draws the three pixel
blue ring inside the edge that the icon buttons draw.

## Props

### onClick

Called when the chip is pressed, by pointer or by keyboard, to start a new
stage.

## Stories

### Default

The chip in Persian at rest, measured against the design, and pressed.

### InEnglish

The same chip in English, the plus on the left.

### Dark

The chip in the dark scheme, in the theme's dark colours.

### Hover

The pointer over the chip: the light grey fill.

### KeyboardFocus

The chip reached with Tab, wearing the focus ring.
