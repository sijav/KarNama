One choice of a set, as a menu item that is a radio: the orders in a column's
sort menu. Its label is the Body text; the chosen one is drawn in blue at
Medium with a tick at the start of the row, and every row keeps the tick's room
so the labels line up. Under the pointer, or with keyboard focus, the row turns
light grey.

## Props

### label

The choice's name.

### selected

Whether it is the chosen one, ticked and read out as checked.

## Stories

### Default

A choice that is not chosen, measured against the design.

### Hover

Under a real pointer, the light grey row. In Storybook itself, hover it
yourself.

### Selected

The chosen one, blue at Medium with its tick.

### SelectedHover

The chosen one under a real pointer, the grey row behind it.

### KeyboardFocus

Reached by Tab, the grey row with the focus ring, and Enter chooses it.

### InEnglish

The chosen one in English, its tick at the left.

### Dark

The chosen one in the dark scheme, in the theme's dark colours.
